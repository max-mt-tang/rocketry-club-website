FOR AUTHORIZED CLUB EDITORS

Club editor

Update announcements, photos, and leadership profiles using your own login. The editing dashboard opens in Pages CMS.

Sign in to edit →

Post an announcement

  1. Open max-mt-tang / rocketry-club-website on the main branch.
  2. Select Announcements, then add an item with a title, date or time, and message. You can also attach a photo and a link.
  3. Turn on Show on website and save. Turn it off to hide an announcement from the page.

Add photos to an album

  1. Select Photo albums and find the matching album.
  2. Add a photo, choose or upload an image, and write a descriptive caption.
  3. Save your changes. Use Homepage gallery for photos displayed on the landing page.

Update leadership profiles

  1. Under Leadership, select 2026–2027 or 2025–2026.
  2. Find the position and edit the person’s name, position description, photo, contact email, school email, or other contact information. Leave optional fields blank to hide them.
  3. Choose or upload a photo, add a photo description, and save. Positions stay in their existing hierarchy on the website.

Changes appear after the website finishes publishing, usually within a few minutes. Photos, announcements, and leadership contact details are public, so only add material intended for the website. Refresh the website after publishing to see your changes.

Editor access

Max manages individual access in Pages CMS. Invited editors can sign in with their own email; repository owners can sign in with GitHub. Contact maxmttang@gmail.com if you need access.
