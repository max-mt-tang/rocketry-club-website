01 / WHO WE ARE

A shared curiosity.
A higher ambition.

We're a team of Interlake students turning a fascination with flight into something we can build with our own hands.

Through design sessions, field tests, and competition launches, we learn by doing—and by helping each other get better. Whether you're a first-time builder or already love engineering, there's a place for you here.
